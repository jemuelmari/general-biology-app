📖 Animal Organ Systems — Detailed

Competency: STEM_BIO11/12-IVa-h-1

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Describe the major animal organ systems and their functions.
  • Explain how animal systems work together to maintain life.
  • Identify key organs of each system.
  • Explain how structure enables function in each system.

🔑 Key Concepts

  • Organ — a group of tissues that work together for a specific function.
  • System integration — organ systems coordinate for the survival of the organism.
  • Surface-area-to-volume ratio — drives specialization in exchange surfaces.

📚 Lesson Content

A. Digestive System

  • Function: Break down food into absorbable nutrients
  • Organs: Mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, liver, pancreas
  • Key adaptations: Villi and microvilli in the small intestine increase surface area for absorption

B. Circulatory System

  • Function: Transport nutrients, gases, hormones, and wastes
  • Organs: Heart, blood vessels (arteries, veins, capillaries), blood
  • Types: Open (insects) vs closed (vertebrates)

C. Respiratory System

  • Function: Exchange O₂ and CO₂ with the environment
  • Organs: Lungs, trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli; gills in fish
  • Key adaptations: Alveoli provide large surface area; thin walls enable diffusion

D. Nervous System

  • Function: Detect stimuli and coordinate rapid responses
  • Organs: Brain, spinal cord, nerves
  • Divisions: Central (CNS) and Peripheral (PNS)

E. Endocrine System

  • Function: Secrete hormones for slower, longer-lasting regulation
  • Glands: Hypothalamus, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal, pancreas, gonads
  • Example: Insulin regulates blood glucose levels

F. Excretory System

  • Function: Remove metabolic wastes and regulate water/salt balance
  • Organs: Kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra
  • Key process: Filtration, reabsorption, secretion, excretion

G. Immune/Lymphatic System

  • Function: Defend against pathogens and remove toxins
  • Organs: Lymph nodes, spleen, thymus, tonsils, WBCs
  • Lines of defense: Physical barriers, innate immunity, adaptive immunity

H. Reproductive System

  • Function: Produce gametes and ensure species survival
  • Organs: Gonads (testes/ovaries), ducts, accessory glands

I. Muscular & Skeletal Systems

  • Function: Support, movement, protection, mineral storage
  • Organs: Bones, cartilage, skeletal muscles, tendons, ligaments

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Each animal organ system has specialized organs for its function.
  • Structures are adapted to maximize efficiency (e.g., alveoli, villi).
  • Systems do not work alone — they coordinate with each other.
  • Integration of systems maintains homeostasis.
