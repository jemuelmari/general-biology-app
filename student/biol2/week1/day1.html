📖 Introduction to Organ Systems and Their Functions

Competency: STEM_BIO11/12-IVa-h-1

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Identify the major organ systems in plants and animals.
  • Describe the primary function of each organ system.
  • Compare how plants and animals perform similar life processes.
  • Recognize that organ systems work together to maintain life.

🔑 Key Concepts

  • Organ System — a group of organs that work together to perform a specific function.
  • Organismal Biology — the study of structure, function, and behavior of whole organisms.
  • Homeostasis — maintenance of a stable internal environment.

📚 Lesson Content

A. Why Organ Systems Matter

Every living organism must perform the same fundamental life processes — but plants and animals do them in very different ways.

E. Comparative Preview

Today we set the stage. Over the next three days, we'll go deeper into how these systems work and how they maintain homeostasis — the body's internal balance.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Plants and animals perform the same basic life processes with different structures.
  • Animals rely on organ systems (circulatory, respiratory, nervous, etc.).
  • Plants rely on shoot and root systems, plus vascular tissues.
  • All systems work together to maintain homeostasis.
